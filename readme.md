Abstract: This paper proposes an enhanced uplink unauthorized random access scheme—Non-Orthogonal Time-Frequency Spatial Index Modulation Multi-Access (NOTFS-IMMA), which deeply integrates Non-Orthogonal Multi-Access (NOMA), Orthogonal Time-Frequency Synchronization (OTFS), and Index Modulation Multi-Access (IMMA). In NOTFS-IMMA, users randomly select delay-Doppler resource blocks based on their own partial data, achieving uncoordinated unauthorized access, effectively enhancing energy efficiency. Theoretical analysis and simulation results show that in terms of bit error rate and spectral efficiency, NOTFS-IMMA significantly outperforms traditional OTFS multi-access and the OTFS-IMMA scheme, providing a superior technical choice for high mobility wireless communication scenarios.
Introduction: Technological Challenges and Research Background of High Mobility Communications. The 6G wireless communication network demands high mobility for terminals and reflective surfaces, and when the carrier frequency is 5GHz, a 1000km/h moving speed produces a Doppler shift of 16.6KHz, posing a severe challenge to the anti-channel fading capability of communication technology. Orthogonal Time-Frequency Space (OTFS) modulation, with its characteristics of mapping information symbols into the delay-Doppler (DD) domain, has become a highly promising technology for high mobility communication scenarios, well adapting to frequency and time selective wireless channels.
Existing OTFS-based multi-access technologies have achieved different resource allocation and performance optimizations, such as user reuse based on delay and Doppler dimensions, and equal distance resource allocation under high delay-Doppler extension environments. Some studies have also combined NOMA with OTFS, based on the mobility characteristics of users to achieve user differentiation. Index Modulation (IM) technology can transmit additional information through transmission resource indices, achieving effective trade-offs between spectrum efficiency, energy efficiency, and bit error rate. The NOTFS-IMMA scheme further enhances the performance of the OTFS system by allowing users to choose DD resource blocks based on information bits.
However, there has not been any research yet to combine uplink OTFS with IMMA and power domain NOMA to reduce the probability of multi-user collisions, enhance spectral efficiency, and optimize bit error rate. In response to this research gap, this paper proposes an unauthorized uplink NOTFS-IMMA system that utilizes a collaborative design of three-dimensional resource selection and power domain NOMA to fill the research gap in this field.
